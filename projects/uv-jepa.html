UV-JEPA

UV-JEPA (Urdu Vision Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) is an ongoing final-year research project investigating how AI can understand relationships between images and Urdu descriptions when paired language resources are constrained.

Research In Progress Vision-Language Urdu NLP FYP · QAU
Research in progress · Repository private during active study Supervisor: Dr. Syed Muhammad Naqi ↗

Overview & Research Motivation

Most prominent vision-language foundation models are trained and benchmarked almost exclusively on English datasets with tens of millions of curated image-text pairs. When applied to lower-resource languages like Urdu, models often struggle due to data scarcity, morphological diversity, and quality variance in existing captions.

UV-JEPA investigates whether latent-predictive architectures inspired by the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) paradigm can learn effective representations between paired images and Urdu captions without requiring massive datasets or relying purely on autoregressive token generation.

Methodology & Architecture

The system operates on paired image and Urdu caption data:

  • Dual Encoders: A vision encoder processes visual content into a feature space, while an Urdu language encoder generates representations of corresponding textual descriptions.
  • Latent-Predictive Learning: Rather than predicting an Urdu sentence token-by-token (which frequently accumulates error and hallucination in low-resource settings), the model predicts a meaning-based representation of the Urdu caption directly in a shared latent space.
  • Benchmarking Baselines: The research directly benchmarks this JEPA-inspired approach against standard contrastive learning (CLIP-style) and autoregressive image captioning baselines under identical Urdu dataset constraints.
  • Data-Regime Experiments: Experiments systematically vary training sample sizes to measure how representation quality scales under low-resource and data-constrained regimes.

Evaluation & Planned Demo

Evaluation focuses primarily on:

  • Cross-Modal Retrieval: Image-to-Urdu text retrieval and Urdu text-to-image retrieval accuracy.
  • Representation Quality: Measuring embedding alignment, stability, and caption reliability.
  • Interactive Web Demo: A lightweight web inference demo is planned to allow users to provide an image and retrieve the most semantically relevant Urdu captions.
